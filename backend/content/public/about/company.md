Hashed Systems is a software studio that designs and builds custom web, mobile
and AI products for growing companies. We work as an embedded product team
rather than a body shop: a typical engagement pairs a product designer, two or
three engineers and a delivery lead who stays with the project from discovery
through to launch.

## What we do

We take on the whole build — discovery, design, engineering, deployment and the
support that follows. Most of our work falls into custom web applications,
cross-platform mobile apps, e-commerce builds, AI and chatbot integration,
UI/UX design, and cloud and DevOps engineering.

## How we are set up

The studio is around 25 people across engineering, design and delivery. Teams
are small and long-lived, so the people who scope your project are the people
who build it. We work in two-week iterations with a demo at the end of each one,
and clients have direct access to the team in a shared channel rather than
through an account manager.

## Where we work

We are fully remote and work with clients across North America, Europe and the
Middle East. Delivery teams overlap with client hours for at least four hours a
day, and we run planning and demos inside that window.

## Technology

Our default stack is TypeScript and React or Next.js on the front end, with
Python (FastAPI, Django) or Node.js behind it, on PostgreSQL. Mobile work is
React Native or Flutter. AI work is built on retrieval-augmented generation with
open and commercial models. Infrastructure runs on AWS, GCP or Azure, described
in Terraform.

## Getting started

Every engagement begins with a scoping call — a free 45-minute conversation
about the problem, the constraints and the deadline. We follow it with a written
proposal that includes scope, timeline and a fixed price. Reach us at
team@hashedsystem.com.
