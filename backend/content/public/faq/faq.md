## How does an engagement start?

With a free 45-minute scoping call. We talk through the problem, the
constraints and the deadline, then send a written proposal with scope, timeline
and a fixed price — usually within three working days.

## How do you price work?

Most projects are fixed-price against an agreed scope, so you know the number
before we start. Longer or less defined engagements can run as a monthly
retainer instead. Support is always a monthly retainer. Every quote comes with a
written scope, and changes to that scope are quoted separately before any work
begins.

## What payment terms do you use?

Fixed-price projects are billed in milestones, typically 30% to start and the
remainder against agreed delivery milestones. Retainers are billed monthly in
advance. Invoices are net 14.

## How long does a project take?

A focused internal tool or design sprint takes a few weeks. A typical web or
mobile application runs two to four months. Larger platform work runs five
months or more. The proposal always contains a dated timeline.

## Who owns the code?

You do. All intellectual property transfers to you on final payment, including
the source code, infrastructure code and design files. We do not retain a
licence and we do not resell client work.

## Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, routinely, and usually before the scoping call if you would prefer.

## Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. Roughly half our engagements are alongside an in-house team — we take a
workstream, or supply specific skills such as mobile or DevOps, and work in your
tools and your process.

## Can you take over a project someone else started?

Usually. We start with a short paid audit of the codebase and infrastructure,
then give you an honest assessment of whether to continue, refactor or rebuild —
including when the answer is that you do not need us.

## What happens after launch?

Every project includes a warranty period during which we fix defects at no
charge. After that, most clients move onto a support retainer for monitoring,
security patching and ongoing improvements.

## Which time zones do you cover?

We are fully remote and overlap with client hours for at least four hours each
working day. Planning sessions and demos are scheduled inside that window.

## How do we stay in touch during the project?

A shared Slack or Teams channel with direct access to the delivery team, a demo
at the end of every two-week iteration, and a written progress summary each
week.
