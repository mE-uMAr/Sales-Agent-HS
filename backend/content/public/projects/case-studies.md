## Logistics dispatch platform

A regional freight company was running dispatch on spreadsheets and phone
calls. We built a web platform for live load assignment, driver tracking and
automated customer notifications, integrated with their existing accounting
system. Dispatch time per load fell from around eleven minutes to under three,
and the platform now handles roughly 1,200 loads a week. Delivered in four
months by a team of four.

## Clinic booking mobile app

A network of physiotherapy clinics needed patients to be able to book, pay and
reschedule without calling reception. We shipped a React Native app for iOS and
Android with calendar sync, card payments and automated reminders. No-show rates
dropped by about a third in the first quarter, and roughly 60% of bookings moved
out of the phone queue. Delivered in three months.

## Support assistant for a SaaS product

A B2B software vendor was losing engineering time to repetitive support
questions. We built a retrieval-augmented assistant over their documentation and
changelog, embedded in the product, with escalation to a human whenever
confidence was low. It now resolves a little over half of incoming questions
without a human, and every escalation arrives with a summary of what the user
already tried. Delivered in six weeks.

## Headless commerce replatform

A specialist retailer had outgrown their hosted storefront: 40,000 SKUs, slow
category pages and no room to customise checkout. We replatformed onto a
headless Next.js storefront with a commerce backend and a CDN edge cache.
Category pages went from roughly 4.2 seconds to under one second, and mobile
conversion rose by 18%. Delivered in three months.

## Cloud migration for a fintech

A payments company needed to leave a single-tenant colocated environment for a
compliant multi-region AWS setup. We described the target in Terraform, migrated
workloads service by service with no scheduled downtime, and left them with
CI/CD, monitoring and a tested disaster-recovery runbook. Delivered in five
months.
